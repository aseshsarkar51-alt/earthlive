A guest book nobody has to hunt for a pen for

Paper guest books sit on a table and mostly collect the same three signatures from people standing nearby. EarthLive's Events feature turns your guest book into something guests actually reach for their phones for — a note, a photo, or a voice message, from anyone at the wedding, all landing in one place you get to keep long after the flowers are gone.

How it works for weddings

Create an Event page for your wedding and set who it's open to — just your guest list, or anyone with the link. Print the QR code on your table cards or program. Guests scan it from their seats, leave a message in whatever form feels right — written, photo, or a voice note — and it's saved the moment they submit it. After the wedding, the page stays exactly as it was, and you can export it as a printable keepsake whenever you want a physical copy.

Questions people ask

Do guests need to download an app?

No — it opens straight in their phone's browser from the QR code or link, same as any website.

Can guests leave a voice message instead of writing something?

Yes, voice notes are one of the entry types, alongside text and photos.

What happens to the page after the wedding — do we lose access?

No, it's yours to keep indefinitely, the same as any other EarthLive page.

Is it private, or can anyone find it?

You control this — most couples keep it open only to their guest list.
