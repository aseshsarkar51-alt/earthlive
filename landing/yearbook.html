The yearbook signing, minus the paper

Every school has one — the notebook that got passed around on the last day, filled with mismatched handwriting and inside jokes half the class has since forgotten. It always ends up in a box somewhere, and you never throw it away. EarthLive's Yearbook/Farewell question set is that notebook, except nobody has to track down a pen, and nothing gets lost between houses.

How it works for graduation

Make your page and pick the Yearbook/Farewell question set — it's built for exactly this, with prompts like “how we met” and “something you'll remember about this place.” Share your link in the class group chat or drop the QR code at your last get-together. Everyone who signs answers in their own words, on their own time, and nothing appears on your page until you've approved it. Once it's full, export it as a printable keepsake if you want a physical copy alongside the digital one.

Questions people ask

Is this instead of my school's official yearbook?

It's a companion to it, not a replacement — the school yearbook is about the class as a whole; this one is specifically what your people would say about you.

Does everyone in my class need their own page, or can we all sign one?

Both work. Most people make their own page and share it with the same group, so everyone ends up with a personal book rather than one shared page.

Does it cost anything?

Free to start. Printed hardcover copies are available if you want a physical version later.

Can I keep it private to just my friend group?

Yes — you choose whether your page is open to anyone with the link or restricted to people you approve.
